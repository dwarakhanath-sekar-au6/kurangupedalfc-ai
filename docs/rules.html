How to play

Fantasy Premier League, explained simply

A clean guide for anyone joining your WhatsApp group, whether they know football or not.

The game in one line: pick a 15-player squad, stay within the budget, choose 11 starters each week, and earn points from real Premier League performances. The official FPL rules say the squad must be 15 players, the total budget must not exceed £100m, and no more than 3 players can come from the same club.

What you do every week

  1. Pick your starting 11 from your 15-man squad.
  2. Choose a captain and vice captain.
  3. Use transfers to fix weak spots or chase upside.
  4. Save chips for the right week, not just any week.

The four big chips

Triple Captain

Your captain scores triple points instead of double for that Gameweek. Best used when your captain has the highest ceiling and a strong fixture.

Bench Boost

Points from your bench count too. Best when your full 15 are in good shape and the bench has real starter-level output.

Wildcard

Lets you rebuild your squad without the normal transfer cost rules. Best when several players need to change at once.

Free Hit

Lets you make a one-week-only squad for a single Gameweek. Good for awkward fixture swings, blanks, or crisis weeks.

What your weekly briefing means

The homepage shows the most important decision first: projected points, the immediate call, the pitch, substitutes, transfer watch, chip watch, and short player notes. It is meant to help you decide quickly without extra noise.

How KuranguPedalFC works

Your squad is treated as a live football team, not a one-time list. The weekly notebook reads your current squad, checks form, minutes, fixtures, ownership, and availability, then recommends the next move. The main team stays as your skeleton; the weekly advice changes.
