How to play

Fantasy Premier League, explained simply

Short rules. Clear steps. No clutter.

The game in one line: pick a 15-player squad, stay within the budget, choose 11 starters each week, and earn points from real Premier League performances.

What happens every week

  1. Pick your starting 11 from the 15-player squad.
  2. Choose a captain and vice captain.
  3. Use transfers only when the move improves the team.
  4. Save chips for the right week.

The four chips

Triple Captain

Your captain scores triple points instead of double for that Gameweek.

Bench Boost

Points from your bench count too.

Wildcard

Lets you rebuild the squad without the normal transfer limits.

Free Hit

Lets you make a one-week-only squad for a single Gameweek.

What KuranguPedalFC shows

Start with the weekly points number, then the immediate call, then the pitch, substitutes, transfer watch, chip watch, and key decisions.

How the app works

Once your access is added, the weekly manager reads your live squad, checks form, fixtures, ownership, and availability, and then recommends the next move.
